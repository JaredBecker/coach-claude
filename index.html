How to read the plan

3–4 × 8–12 means 3 to 4 sets of 8 to 12 reps. Do the reps, rest a minute or two, then repeat for 3 or 4 rounds total.

3s down, 3s up is the tempo for each single rep: three seconds lowering, three seconds back up. Slow and controlled is the whole point with a tendon, it keeps the load steady and stops momentum doing the work for you.

Making it hard enough: with no weights, the load comes from going single-leg, slowing the tempo, and adding range. Once an exercise feels easy for all its reps, progress it: load a backpack with books or water bottles, drop to one leg, or go deeper. Aim for the last couple of reps to feel genuinely hard but still clean, with pain no higher than 3/10. The mini loop bands add the resistance on the hip work (bridges, clamshells, lateral walks).
